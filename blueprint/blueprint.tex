\title[Blueprint of the Lean formalization]{Regularity of the gradient for a class of nonlinear possibly degenerate elliptic equations: blueprint of the Lean formalization}
\author{Juan J. Manfredi}

\begin{document}
\begin{abstract}
This document collects the natural-language statements and detailed proofs that accompany the Lean~4 formalization of the numbered lemmas and theorems of J.~J.~Manfredi, \emph{Regularity of the gradient for a class of nonlinear possibly degenerate elliptic equations}, Ph.D.\ thesis, Washington University in St.~Louis, 1986.
Seven results that the thesis quotes from the literature, (C1)--(C7), are not proved; they are stated precisely and carried as explicit hypotheses (the \texttt{Cited...} declarations). Everything else is proved.
Each subsection corresponds to the Lean declaration of the same name in the directory \texttt{ManfrediThesis/}; cross-references point to the results that a proof uses.
Section~\ref{sec:main} contains the formalized results of the thesis, in the order of the thesis.
Within the other sections, items appear in dependency order.
\end{abstract}
