\begin{definition}
Let $n\ge2$, $p>1$, $0\le\varepsilon\le1$, $0<\lambda\le\Lambda$ and $a>0$, let
$\Omega\subseteq\R^n$ and let $f\colon\R^n\times\R\times\R^n\to\R$.  We say that
$f$ satisfies the \emph{growth condition} (F1) of the thesis on $\Omega$ with these
parameters, written
$\mathrm{VariationalGrowthF1}(n,p,\varepsilon,\lambda,\Lambda,a,\Omega,f)$, if
$f$ is a Carath\'eodory function ($x\mapsto f(x,z,h)$ measurable for each
$(z,h)$, $(z,h)\mapsto f(x,z,h)$ continuous for each $x$) and
\[
-a+\lambda(\varepsilon+|h|^2)^{p/2}\;\le\;f(x,z,h)\;\le\;\Lambda(\varepsilon+|h|^2)^{p/2}+a
\]
for every $x\in\Omega$, $z\in\R$, $h\in\R^n$.  This is condition (F1) of the thesis
alone, which is all that the De Giorgi--Ladyzhenskaya--Ural'tseva H\"older
estimates of Giaquinta and Giusti require; the stronger package
(F1) of the thesis--(F3) of the thesis is packaged separately.
\end{definition}
