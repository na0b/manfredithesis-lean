\begin{definition}
Let $1\le p<\infty$ and $\Omega\subseteq\R^n$. We write $f\in W^{1,p}_0(\Omega)$
\emph{with weak gradient $g$}, denoted $\mathrm{MemW1pZeroOn}(n,p,\Omega,f,g)$,
if $f\in W^{1,p}(\Omega)$ with weak gradient $g$ (\noderef{MemW1pOn}) and $f$ is
the limit, in the $W^{1,p}(\Omega)$ seminorms, of a sequence of smooth functions
compactly supported in $\Omega$: there are
$\varphi_k\in C^\infty(\R^n)$ with compact support contained in $\Omega$ such
that
\[
\int_\Omega|f-\varphi_k|^p\,dx\longrightarrow 0,
\qquad
\int_\Omega|g-\nabla\varphi_k|^p\,dx\longrightarrow 0
\qquad(k\to\infty),
\]
where $|g-\nabla\varphi_k|^p=\bigl(\sum_{i=1}^n|g^i-D_i\varphi_k|^2\bigr)^{p/2}$.
This is the usual space $W^{1,p}_0(\Omega)$, the closure of $C_0^\infty(\Omega)$
in $W^{1,p}(\Omega)$, referred to in \S1 and used for the boundary conditions of
\S3--\S5.
\end{definition}
