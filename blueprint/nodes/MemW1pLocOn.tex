\begin{definition}
Let $1\le p<\infty$ and $\Omega\subseteq\R^n$. We write
$f\in W^{1,p}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\Omega)$ \emph{with weak gradient $g$}, denoted
$\mathrm{MemW1pLocOn}(n,p,\Omega,f,g)$, if $g$ is the weak gradient of $f$ on
$\Omega$ in the sense of \noderef{HasWeakGradientOn}, and for every compact
$K\subseteq\Omega$ one has $f\in L^p(K)$ and $|g|\in L^p(K)$. This is the local
Sobolev space of \S1 of the paper: the functions in $\Omega$ which are in
$W^{1,p}(K)$ for each compact $K\subseteq\Omega$.
\end{definition}
