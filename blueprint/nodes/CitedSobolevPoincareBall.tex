\begin{definition}
Let $n\ge2$.  The predicate $\mathrm{CitedSobolevPoincareBall}(n)$ is the cited
result (C6) of the Note for the formalization (Sobolev--Poincar\'e inequality on
balls):

\emph{there is a constant $C=C(n)>0$ such that for every ball $B_\rho(x_0)$ and
every $g\in W^{1,1}(B_\rho(x_0))$ with weak gradient $\nabla g$
(\noderef{MemW1pOn}),}
\[
\|g-g_{B_\rho}\|_{L^{n/(n-1)}(B_\rho)}\;\le\;C\int_{B_\rho}|\nabla g|\,dx ,
\qquad
g_{B_\rho}=\frac{1}{|B_\rho|}\int_{B_\rho}g\,dx .
\]
The left-hand side is written out as
$\bigl(\int_{B_\rho}|g-g_{B_\rho}|^{n/(n-1)}dx\bigr)^{(n-1)/n}$.  The constant is
bound before the ball and the function, so it genuinely depends only on $n$.
This is used as an explicit hypothesis in Lemmas~Lemma~2.2 of the thesis and
Lemma~4.2 of the thesis, where the paper derives them from it, and is never introduced as
an axiom.
\end{definition}
