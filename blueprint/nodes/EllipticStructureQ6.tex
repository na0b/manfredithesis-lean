\begin{definition}
Let $n\ge2$, $p>1$, $\Omega\subseteq\R^n$, let
$A\colon\R^n\times\R\times\R^n\to\R^n$ and let $\sigma\colon\R\to\R$.  We say
that $A$ satisfies condition (Q6) of the thesis on $\Omega$ with modulus $\sigma$,
written $\mathrm{EllipticStructureQ6}(n,p,\Omega,A,\sigma)$, if $\sigma$ is
bounded, continuous and increasing with $\sigma(0)=0$, and for all
$x,x'\in\Omega$, all $z,z'\in\R$ and all $h\in\R^n$,
\[
|A(x,z,h)-A(x',z',h)|\;\le\;\sigma(|x-x'|+|z-z'|)\,(1+|h|^2)^{\frac{p-1}2}.
\]
The modulus $\sigma$ is carried as an explicit parameter because Theorems~5
and~6 quantify their constants over ``the function $\sigma$ given in condition
(Q6)''.
\end{definition}
