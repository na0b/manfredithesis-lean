\begin{definition}
Let $\Omega\subseteq\R^n$, let $G^1,\dots,G^n\colon\R^n\to\R^n$ be the component
weak gradients of a mapping $F$ (so that $DF(x)_{ij}=G^i(x)^j$ is the formal
differential and $J_F(x)=\det DF(x)$), and let
$\tau\colon\R^n\to\R^{n\times n}$.  We say that $\tau$ is \emph{the distortion
matrix} of $F$ on $\Omega$, written $\mathrm{IsDistortionMatrix}(n,\Omega,G,\tau)$,
if for every $x\in\Omega$
\[
\tau^{-1}(x)=
\begin{cases}
J_F(x)^{-\frac2n}\,D^tF(x)\,DF(x) & \text{if }J_F(x)>0,\\
I & \text{otherwise.}
\end{cases}
\]
In the first case the condition is written multiplicatively and without matrix
inversion, as
$\tau(x)\,\bigl(D^tF(x)DF(x)\bigr)=J_F(x)^{2/n}I$, which is equivalent to it.
This is the distortion matrix of thesis p.~52.
\end{definition}
