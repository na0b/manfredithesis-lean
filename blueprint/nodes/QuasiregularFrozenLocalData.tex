\begin{lemma}[The local data of a quasiregular map on an interposed set]
Let $n\ge2$, $K\ge1$, let $\Omega,\Omega''\subseteq\R^n$ be open with
$\overline{\Omega''}\subseteq\Omega$ and $\overline{\Omega''}$ compact.  Let
$F\colon\R^n\to\R^n$ be $K$-quasiregular on $\Omega$ with component gradients
$G$ (\noderef{IsQuasiregularOn}), let $\tau$ be its distortion matrix
(\noderef{IsDistortionMatrix}), assume the cited result (C7)
(\noderef{CitedQuasiregularBasics}) at $n,K$ for the data $F,G,\tau$ on
\emph{every} open $\Omega_0\subseteq\Omega$, and assume that there is no
$c\in\R^n$ with $F=c$ almost everywhere on $\Omega$.  Let
$\tau'\colon\R^n\to\R^{n\times n}$ satisfy $\tau=\tau'$ almost everywhere on
$\Omega$, let $f^0$ be an integrand with
$f^0(x,z,h)=(\tau'(x)h,h)^{n/2}$ for every $x\in\Omega''$ and all $z\in\R$,
$h\in\R^n$, and fix $i\in\{1,\dots,n\}$.  Write $u=F^i$ and $g=G^i$.  Then
there are reals $M,W_n\ge0$ with
\[
u\in W^{1,n}(\Omega'')\ \text{with weak gradient}\ g
\quad(\noderef{MemW1pOn}),\qquad
|u|\le M\ \text{almost everywhere on }\Omega'',
\]
\[
\int_{\Omega''}|u|^{\,n}+\int_{\Omega''}\|g\|^{\,n}\le W_n^{\,n},
\qquad
\mathrm{IsLocalMinimumOn}\ n\ n\ \Omega''\ f^0\ u\ g
\quad(\noderef{IsLocalMinimumOn}).
\]
\end{lemma}
\begin{proof}
\textbf{(1) The local minimum on $\Omega''$.}  First, $F$ is $K$-quasiregular on
$\Omega''$ with component gradients $G$, and $\tau$ is its distortion matrix
there.  Indeed \noderef{IsQuasiregularOn} is the conjunction of
$F^j\in W^{1,n}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\Omega)$ with weak gradient $G^j$ for every $j$
and of an almost-everywhere inequality guarded by $x\in\Omega$: the first
transfers to $\Omega''$ by clause (2) of \noderef{WeakGradientRestrict}, applied
with $q=n$ and the open sets $\Omega\supseteq\Omega''$ --- its guards being
$1\le n$, $1\le n$, the openness of both sets and the inclusion
$\Omega''\subseteq\Omega$, which follows from
$\Omega''\subseteq\overline{\Omega''}\subseteq\Omega$ --- and the second because
$\Omega''\subseteq\Omega$, so the guard $x\in\Omega''$ implies the guard
$x\in\Omega$ and the exceptional null set only shrinks.  And
\noderef{IsDistortionMatrix} is a statement of the form ``for every
$x\in\Omega$, \dots'', so it restricts to $\Omega''\subseteq\Omega$ at once.

Now apply the (C7) hypothesis at $\Omega_0=\Omega''$, legitimate since
$\Omega''$ is open and $\Omega''\subseteq\Omega$, and feed it the two
restrictions just obtained.  Its clause (4) gives
\[
\mathrm{IsLocalMinimumOn}\ n\ n\ \Omega''\
\bigl(\textstyle(x,z,h)\mapsto(\tau(x)h,h)^{n/2}\bigr)\ u\ g
\tag{3.1}
\]
(\noderef{IsLocalMinimumOn}).  \emph{This is the one clause of \textup{(C7)}
taken at $\Omega''$ rather than at $\Omega$, and it is why the hypothesis is
assumed in the universal form.}

Next, (3.1) holds with $f^0$ in place of the $\tau$-integrand.  By
\noderef{IsLocalMinimumOn} the assertion (3.1) is the conjunction of
$u\in W^{1,n}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\Omega'')$ with weak gradient $g$, which does not
mention the integrand at all, of the integrability on
$\operatorname{tsupp}\varphi$ of \emph{both} of the integrands appearing below,
and of the family of inequalities
\[
\int_{\operatorname{tsupp}\varphi}f^0\bigl(x,u(x),g(x)\bigr)dx
\le\int_{\operatorname{tsupp}\varphi}
f^0\bigl(x,u(x)+\varphi(x),g(x)+g_\varphi(x)\bigr)dx
\]
over all $\varphi$ with $\varphi\in W^{1,n}(\Omega'')$ of weak gradient
$g_\varphi$ (\noderef{MemW1pOn}), $\varphi$ of compact support and
$\operatorname{tsupp}\varphi\subseteq\Omega''$.  Each such integral is taken
over $\operatorname{tsupp}\varphi\subseteq\Omega''\subseteq\Omega$, and on
$\Omega''$ the hypothesis on $f^0$ gives
$f^0(x,z,h)=(\tau'(x)h,h)^{n/2}$, while $\tau'=\tau$ almost everywhere on
$\Omega$ by hypothesis; hence for almost every $x$ in the domain of integration
the two integrands agree pointwise, for all values of their second and third
arguments.

This settles both of the clauses that mention the integrand, by the same
observation.  For the integrability clause: (3.1) supplies, for each admissible
$\varphi$, the integrability on $\operatorname{tsupp}\varphi$ of
$x\mapsto(\tau(x)g(x),g(x))^{n/2}$ and of
$x\mapsto(\tau(x)(g+g_\varphi)(x),(g+g_\varphi)(x))^{n/2}$; each of the two
$f^0$-integrands agrees with the corresponding $\tau$-integrand at almost every
point of $\operatorname{tsupp}\varphi$ by the previous paragraph, and
integrability on a set is preserved under altering the function on a null subset
of it, so both $f^0$-integrands are integrable on $\operatorname{tsupp}\varphi$
as well.  For the inequality clause: integrals of functions agreeing almost
everywhere on the domain of integration are equal, so each of the two sides is
unchanged by the substitution.  Hence all three clauses hold for $f^0$ and
$\mathrm{IsLocalMinimumOn}\ n\ n\ \Omega''\ f^0\ u\ g$.

\textbf{(2) The Sobolev membership, $M$ and $W_n$.}  By clause (1) of
\noderef{IsQuasiregularOn}, $u\in W^{1,n}_{\mathrm{loc}}(\Omega)$ with weak
gradient $g$ (\noderef{MemW1pLocOn}).  By clause (4) of
\noderef{WeakGradientRestrict}, applied with $q=n$ and the open sets
$\Omega\supseteq\Omega''$ --- whose hypotheses are $1\le n$, $1\le n$, the
openness of both, $\Omega''\subseteq\Omega$, that local membership,
$\overline{\Omega''}$ compact and $\overline{\Omega''}\subseteq\Omega$, the last
two by hypothesis --- we get
\[
u\in W^{1,n}(\Omega'')\ \text{with weak gradient}\ g
\tag{3.2}
\]
(\noderef{MemW1pOn}).  For the sup bound, apply the (C7) hypothesis at
$\Omega_0=\Omega$, legitimate since $\Omega$ is open and $\Omega\subseteq\Omega$,
and feed it the present \noderef{IsQuasiregularOn} and
\noderef{IsDistortionMatrix} hypotheses; its clause (1) supplies a field
$\widetilde F$ with $\widetilde F$ continuous on $\Omega$ and
$F=\widetilde F$ almost everywhere on $\Omega$.  \emph{Clause \textup{(1)} does
not say that $F$ is continuous, and that is why the representative is used
here}: $F$ is carried by \noderef{IsQuasiregularOn} only as a
$W^{1,n}_{\mathrm{loc}}$ map, determined up to a null set, so continuity is a
property of $\widetilde F$ and not of $F$.  Its $i$-th component
$\widetilde F^i$ is continuous on $\Omega$, hence on $\overline{\Omega''}$,
which is a compact subset of $\Omega$ by hypothesis; a continuous real function
on a compact set is bounded, so
\[
M=\sup_{\overline{\Omega''}}\bigl|\widetilde F^i\bigr|<\infty,
\qquad\text{and}\qquad 0\le M,
\]
the nonnegativity because $M$ is a supremum of absolute values, taken to be $0$
in the degenerate case $\overline{\Omega''}=\emptyset$.  Transport the bound to
$u$: $F=\widetilde F$ almost everywhere on $\Omega$, hence componentwise, and
$\Omega''\subseteq\Omega$, so $u=F^i=\widetilde F^i$ almost everywhere on
$\Omega''$ and therefore $|u|\le M$ almost everywhere on $\Omega''$.

By (3.2) and \noderef{MemW1pOn}, $u$ and $g$ lie in $L^n(\Omega'')$, so
$\int_{\Omega''}|u|^n+\int_{\Omega''}\|g\|^n$ is a finite nonnegative number;
put $W_n$ equal to its $n$-th root, so that $0\le W_n$ and
$\int_{\Omega''}|u|^n+\int_{\Omega''}\|g\|^n\le W_n^{\,n}$.
\end{proof}
