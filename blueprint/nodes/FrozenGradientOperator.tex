\begin{definition}
Let $f\colon\R^n\times\R\times\R^n\to\R$, let $x_0\in\R^n$ and $z\in\R$, and put
$f^0(h)=f(x_0,z,h)$.  The \emph{frozen gradient operator}
$\mathrm{FrozenGradientOperator}(n,f,x_0,z)$ is the vector field
\[
A\colon\R^n\to\R^n,\qquad A(h)=\nabla_hf^0(h),\quad\text{i.e.}\quad A^i(h)=D_{h_i}f^0(h) .
\]
This is the field $A$ introduced on thesis p.~32, whose equation
$\operatorname{div}A(\nabla v)=0$ is the Euler equation (4.1) of the thesis of the
frozen functional $F^0$.  By (F2) of the thesis it satisfies (4.2) of the thesis, i.e.\
(Q1) of the thesis--(Q2) of the thesis with $\gamma_0=\nu_0$, $\gamma_1=\nu_1$.
\end{definition}
