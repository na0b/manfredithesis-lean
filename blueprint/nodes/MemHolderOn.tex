\begin{definition}
Let $0\le\eta$, let $\Omega\subseteq\R^n$ and let $u\colon\R^n\to\R$.  We write
$u\in C^\eta(\Omega)$, denoted $\mathrm{MemHolderOn}(n,\eta,\Omega,u)$, if $u$ is
continuous on $\Omega$ and there is $C\ge0$ with
\[
|u(x)-u(y)|\le C\,|x-y|^{\eta}\qquad\text{for all }x,y\in\Omega .
\]
This is the H\"older class $C^{0,\eta}$ of \S4, taken on the set $\Omega$ (for us
always a closed ball, a sphere, or the closure $\overline{\Omega'}$ of a
Lipschitz domain).  For $\eta=0$ the condition is continuity together with
bounded oscillation.
\end{definition}
